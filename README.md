### About me

I'm an ML engineer based in Boston. I build production LLM systems, RAG pipelines, and work on making models actually usable at scale.

Currently at **Community Dreams Foundation** working on causal inference and data pipelines. Before that I was at **Nevina Infotech** where I built forecasting systems that served 50K+ daily predictions in production.

I did my M.Sc. in Data Analytics at **Northeastern University** with a concentration in machine learning, and my B.Tech. at **KL University** in electronics and communication.

When I'm not training models I'm probably figuring out why they broke in production.
